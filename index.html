How it works

One workspace. Every step, recorded. A single file that is your proof.

Your workspace

Research, manage files, and work alongside an AI research assistant built into the workspace — so even your thinking gets captured in the record.

2

Every step, captured

As you work, the platform records the exact steps you took to reach a result — what changed, and when.

3

One sealed record

It all becomes a complete, tamper-evident record of the entire lifespan of your work — verifiable at any time, robust evidence that you did it, when you did it.

A promise that can't be broken

WorkSigned is built as an Equitable Corporation — a company structure we designed so its core promises are locked at inception, not a policy that can be quietly revised later. What you see below cannot be bought away, restructured, or walked back — by us, or by anyone who comes after.

Your data stays yours

Never sold. Never licensed. Never used for anything you didn't ask for. You own what you produce — and the record of it.

Protected from takeovers

No investor, no matter how large, can ever gain enough control to rewrite the rules or extract value from your work. The structure prevents it by design.

Rules that can't change

The core commitments are locked at inception. A future majority vote, a new board, or a change of ownership cannot quietly revise them away.
