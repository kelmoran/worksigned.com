How it works

One workspace. Every step, recorded. A single file that is your proof.

1

Your workspace

Research, manage files, and work in a complete workspace — documents, code, drawing, chat, and email, all in one place. Everything you do in it is captured in the record.

2

Every step, captured

As you work, the platform records the exact steps you took to reach a result — what changed, and when.

3

One sealed record

It all becomes a complete, tamper-evident record of the entire lifespan of your work — verifiable at any time, robust evidence that you did it, when you did it.

One platform. Every tool you work in.

Code, documents, spreadsheets, presentations, LaTeX, and AI chat — not bolted-on plugins, but native tools that all write to the same signed record. And code you write in the editor runs right in the platform.

A promise that can't be walked back

WorkSigned is built as an Equitable Corporation — a company structure we designed so its core promises are locked at inception, not a policy that can be quietly revised later. The platform is user-auditable: public logs and open financials mean you can check the commitments for yourself, not just take our word for them. What you see below cannot be bought away or walked back — by us, or by anyone who comes after.

Your data stays yours

Today, the data a platform's users generate becomes the company's asset — sold, licensed, and traded behind terms of service no one reads.

The fix: User data is never sold, mined, licensed, or read. What you produce is yours, full stop.

It can never be bought away

Companies are sold to private equity or large acquirers, and the terms that protected users are quietly rewritten.

The fix: A hard prohibition on any sale to private equity or venture capital. No outside investor can ever gain controlling power — the company is structurally independent.

The rules can't be walked back

A company's privacy and data promises are only as good as its next board meeting. Terms get revised, policies get walked back.

The fix: The core commitments are locked at inception and written into the founding documents. A future board or majority vote cannot reverse them.

Auditable by you

Corporate decisions are made behind closed doors. Reports are audited once a year by people who report to management.

The fix: A user-auditable platform: public server logs and public financial reports, with every API call and transaction open to audit.

Why this, why now

The flow of data and credit is drifting toward the few. We built WorkSigned on a simple insistence: the people who create the work should keep proof of it — and the promise should be one that cannot be bought away.
