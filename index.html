Securing the entire research supply chain

Every stage of research is where intent can leak, work can be exposed, and credit can be disputed. WorkSigned is built around the process — and because we control the toolchain, each stage gets privacy, protection, and proof. Proof of work spans the whole thing; the privacy and protection are tuned to what each stage actually risks.

1

Explore without revealing intent

A single search like “CRISPR editing a gene linked to a rare genetic disease” tells a competitor exactly what you’re planning — before you’ve told anyone. WorkSigned’s stealth mode shards your query into harmless parts, disperses them across separate search engines, AI providers, and IP addresses, then synthesizes the result privately. Your intent is never leaked.

Stealth research mode

Search the web without revealing intent

Even the act of searching is a leak. Stealth mode gets you real results from the open web while your intent never surfaces as a pattern.

  • Queries are sharded into harmless parts.
  • Dispersed across engines, providers & IPs — no single trace.
  • Privately synthesized into a full result.
  • Logged in your signed record — without exposing intent.
2

Collect from many sources, quietly

WorkSigned aggregates data across many sources with query translation so no single request exposes the shape of your project. Because you control the entire toolchain, you can choose where data flows — and keep the destination and the intent hidden from third parties.

3

Process locally & privately

Preliminary and sensitive results are processed on your or our private LLMs — never routed to frontier models that train on your data. Every agent and process runs sandboxed, every binary on the platform is analyzed for telemetry, and monitoring watches for exfiltration. What touches your data is known, contained, and audited.

4

The entire process, signed — and attributed

Everything you did, in order, becomes a tamper-evident, hash-chained record — verifiable in seconds by a journal, funder, or tribunal. And it goes beyond “the work happened”: it names which person did which part, and when, so authorship and priority are provable down to the individual.

The Work Ledger acts as both the proof that the work was done, how it was done, and at what cost.

Every project keeps one signed ledger. Zoom out and it’s the whole story; zoom in and it’s every keystroke, mouse move, and dollar that built the work.

Service guarantees that can't be walked back

Privacy and proof you can only trust if the company making them can’t be bought. WorkSigned is built as an Equitable Corporation — a structure we designed so its core guarantees are locked at inception, not a policy that can be quietly revised later. The platform is user-auditable: public logs and open financials mean you can check the commitments for yourself.

Your data stays yours

Today, the data a platform’s users generate becomes the company’s asset — sold, licensed, and traded behind terms no one reads.

The fix: User data is never sold, mined, licensed, or read. What you produce is yours, full stop.

It can never be bought away

Companies are sold to private equity or large acquirers, and the terms that protected users are quietly rewritten.

The fix: A hard prohibition on any sale to private equity or venture capital. No outside investor can gain controlling power.

The rules can’t be walked back

A company’s privacy promises are only as good as its next board meeting. Terms get revised, policies get walked back.

The fix: The core commitments are locked at inception and written into the founding documents. A future board cannot reverse them.

Auditable by you

Corporate decisions are made behind closed doors. Reports are audited once a year by people who report to management.

The fix: A user-auditable platform: public server logs and public financial reports, every API call and transaction open to audit.
