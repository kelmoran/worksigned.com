Privacy, protection & proof for the entire research process.

WorkSigned protects the entirety of the research journey, from beginning to end. Our tool-rich research platform shields the researcher’s intent while the work is explored, secures the work as it is built, and produces a tamper-evident record that attributes the result to the people who made it. WorkSigned operates as an Equitable Corporation — its record verifies independently of who owns the company, and a charter-level bar plus a per-signee remedy make capture of the company, its IP, or its terms expensive and public.

Securing the entire research supply chain

Every stage of research is where intent can leak, work can be exposed, and credit can be disputed. WorkSigned is built around the process — and because we control the toolchain, each stage gets privacy, protection, and proof. Proof of work spans the whole thing; the privacy and protection are tuned to what each stage actually risks.

1

Explore without revealing intent

A single search like “CRISPR editing a gene linked to a rare genetic disease” tells a competitor exactly what the researcher is planning — before the work has been told to anyone. WorkSigned’s protected search shards the query into harmless parts, disperses them across separate search engines, AI providers, and IP addresses, then synthesizes the result privately. The researcher’s intent is never leaked.

Protected search

Search the web without revealing intent

Even the act of searching is a leak. Protected search returns real results from the open web while the intent never surfaces as a pattern.

  • Queries are sharded into harmless parts.
  • Dispersed across engines, providers & IPs — no single trace.
  • Privately synthesized into a full result.
  • Logged in the signed record — without exposing intent.
2

Collect from many sources, quietly

WorkSigned aggregates data across many sources with query translation so no single request exposes the shape of the project. Because the institution controls the entire toolchain, it can choose where data flows — and keep the destination and the intent hidden from third parties.

3

Process locally & privately

Preliminary and sensitive results are processed on the institution’s hosted LLMs by default — and optionally on WorkSigned’s private LLMs — on models that do not train on the institution’s data. Every agent and process runs sandboxed, every binary on the platform is analyzed for telemetry, and monitoring watches for exfiltration. What touches the institution’s data is known, contained, and audited.

4

The entire process, signed — and attributed

Everything the researcher did, in order, becomes a tamper-evident, hash-chained record — independently verifiable by a journal, funder, or institution. And it goes beyond “the work happened”: it names which person did which part, and when, so authorship and priority are provable down to the individual.

The WorkSigned Record acts as the proof that the work was done — how and at what cost.

Every project keeps one WorkSigned Record. Zoom out and it’s the whole story; zoom in and it’s every keystroke, mouse move, and dollar that built the work.

The same one day, in detail. The platform captures the work as it happens — edits, saves, terminal commands, and spend — and folds the whole day into a single hash.

17:55 end of day 412 events + 6 files → folded into one snapshot 5a88…b1f2

The events are recorded in the order they happen and rolled up into one hash per day — a single SHA-256 of the day’s files and activity, signed with the date and the researcher’s identity and linked to the previous day. A back-dated file would leave no record of the activity that produced it.

Every entry is append-only & hash-chained — work, milestones, and spend in one signed record. Nothing can be silently altered — any change breaks the chain and is detectable. It’s the research timeline and the grant ledger — no separate accounting software.

Guarantees that outlive a change of ownership

Proof that holds up no matter what happens to the company. WorkSigned operates as an Equitable Corporation: the WorkSigned Record is a hash chain, signed by WorkSigned and anchored by an independent daily time-stamp (or the institution’s own key), so a sale, a change of ownership, or even our failure does not invalidate, re-price, or re-purpose what has already been signed. On top of that, a charter-level bar and a per-signee remedy make capture costly and public. The platform is user-auditable: every line of deployed code is auditable by the institution’s IT security team, and the financials and cap table are published.

The record outlives the company

A signed record is only durable if it can be checked after the fact — long after the company that made it has changed hands or ceased to exist.

The fix: The WorkSigned Record is a hash chain signed by WorkSigned and anchored by an independent daily time-stamp (and, in the institution-hosted model, by the institution’s own key). It verifies independently of the issuer — a capture, a sale, or our failure cannot invalidate or re-price it. This is the layer no acquirer can touch.

It can’t be quietly bought away

Companies are sold to private equity or large acquirers, and the terms that protected users are quietly rewritten.

The fix: The charter requires a supermajority and a right of first refusal for any sale of control, the core IP, or the brand to a private equity firm, a venture capital fund, or any acquirer that would control the board. A capture is priced and public — not possible to do quietly.

The terms can only get stronger

A company’s privacy promises are only as good as its next board meeting. Terms get revised, policies get walked back.

The fix: The Equitable Terms can only be strengthened, never weakened — a material adverse change to data ownership, auditability, or the no-extraction rule requires the same supermajority and triggers the per-signee remedy (termination, data return, escrow release, and the record remains valid). So no future board, owner, or terms update can quietly walk the rules back.

Auditable by the institution

Corporate decisions are made behind closed doors. Reports are audited once a year by people who report to management.

The fix: A user-auditable platform: every line of deployed code is auditable by the institution’s IT security team, and the company’s financials and cap table are published so the commitments can be checked.
